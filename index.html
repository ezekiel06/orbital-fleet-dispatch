Orbital Fleet Dispatch

A simulator for AI data centers in orbit. It models how much computing a fleet of solar-powered GPU satellites can deliver, and how much better scheduling gets back, without dropping any battery below its safety floor. It runs on real orbits, live space weather and real satellite telemetry.
