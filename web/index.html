How it works. Real orbits (CelesTrak data for 12 Starlink satellites; computed orbits for the 650 km cases). Bit-flip rates calibrated from Google's published TPU radiation tests; the South Atlantic Anomaly is modeled as a box with 5× the rate. Batteries never drain below the safety floor; GPUs throttle at 80 °C. Every extra kWh is assumed sold at today's GPU rental prices.

Not modeled. Hot spots (the whole satellite has one temperature), laser-link bandwidth limits, ground-station handoffs, and degradation over years.

Open-source simulator, MIT license. Sources: CelesTrak, NOAA SWPC, Google Project Suncatcher, ESA OPS-SAT, SatNOGS.
